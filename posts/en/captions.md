## 02-spot (carousel, 5 slides)

Five waters, each with its own character.

🌾 Village pond: shallow, muddy, crucian carp and rudd. The best place for your first rod.
🌲 Quiet lake: pines, a fire lookout over the woods and a log cabin with a boat by the jetty.
🏰 Castle lake: deep, stony and clear. Pike, zander and trout hunt here.
☢️ Pripyat: a reservoir under the plant's stacks. Fish up to 40 kg, if you dare.
🌉 Old bridge: a strong current, snags and eddies. Home of a 60 kg catfish.

## 03-fish (carousel, 2 slides)

21 kinds of fish, from a tiny bleak to a 60 kg catfish.

Each bites its own way: a perch grabs at once, a bream fiddles with the bait for ages, a catfish tugs and goes quiet. The sterlet is in the Red Book, so it can only be let go.

And there are three secrets. The odds are 1 in 25,000. Seen one yet? 👀

## 04-rods

From a willow switch to a catfish rod.

You start with a bendy willow switch that holds fish up to 600 g. Then hazel, bamboo, telescopic, fibreglass, carbon and a carp rod. The catfish rod takes fish up to 40 kg.

You can land a heavier fish on a lighter rod, but you'll have to work for it. Which one are you on? 🎣

## 05-sky

No two evenings alike.

Every sunset and dawn has its own colours: golden, crimson, raspberry, fiery, lilac. The sky is the same for every angler at the same moment, so if tonight is raspberry, everyone sees it.

What's yours like tonight? 🌅

## 06-storm

A storm over the old bridge. ⛈️

Rain is rare in Doberega and storms are rarer still: lightning, a flash across the whole sky and thunder rolling over the river. But in a downpour the fish bite twice as eagerly.

So when it thunders, it's time to cast.

Night on the water. 🌙

Deep in the night strange things happen on the water: pale lights over the far shore, a boat with no oars, eyes right by the bank. It doesn't change the catch. Probably.

Those who stay till night know. What have you seen?
